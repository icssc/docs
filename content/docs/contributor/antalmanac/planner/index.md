## About

AntAlmanac Planner is a multi-year course planner and discovery tool for classes at UC Irvine. Features include:

- **Show requirements** for multiple majors and minors             
- **View completion** of your _major_, _specialization_, _minor_, and _GE_ requirements
- **Import** your unofficial transcript via [StudentAccess](https://www.reg.uci.edu/access/student/transcript/?seg=U) to populate previous courses.
- **Add credits** from any _transferred courses_, _AP exams_, and _GE/Elective credits_
- **Search** for courses and instructors to find reviews, grade distributions, and more.
