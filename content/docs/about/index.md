## What is ICSSC?

The ICS Student Council is a far-reaching organization that aims to improve student life within and beyond the Donald Bren School of Information and Computer Sciences.

Check out [our website](https://icssc.club) and feel free to join us at our [events](https://icssc.club/events). All UCI affiliates are welcome.

## What is ICSSC Projects?

Projects is one of ICSSC's six committees. We work in dedicated teams on apps and services to support the ICS community and the UCI population as a whole. From user research and design to full-stack development, we also strengthen our members' professional skills and serve as an excellent example of a modern technical organization.

All our projects are open-source, so you can see our work on our [GitHub](https://github.com/icssc/) and we encourage contributions.

We hope to support students in the best way possible, so if you have any feedback please join the [Projects Discord](https://discord.gg/GzF76D7UhY)!

## How do I get involved?

If you're a UCI student, start using our projects!
- Plan your degree and create your schedule with [AntAlmanac](https://antalmanac.com/)
- Schedule team meetings with [ZotMeet](https://zotmeet.com/)
- Decide where to eat on campus with [PeterPlate](https://peterplate.com/)

If you're a developer, check out [Anteater API](/docs/developer/anteaterapi).

If you want to contribute, check out the [contributor docs](/docs/contributor), or learn more about joining ICSSC [on our website](https://icssc.club).
